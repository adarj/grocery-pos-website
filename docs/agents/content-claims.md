# Content-claims guide for agents

Read [capability claims](../product/public-capability-claims.md) and
[Website vs Manager](../product/website-vs-manager.md) before writing product copy.

For each customer-facing capability claim:

1. Verify separate authoritative publication/disclosure approval for the information
   and public audience, plus product-status evidence and its market/version and
   limitations. Maturity labels, including INTERNAL, do not authorize disclosure;
   absent, unclear, or conflicting approval means withhold.
2. Use the evidenced AVAILABLE, PILOT, PREVIEW, PLANNED, or INTERNAL treatment.
   Keep maturity/access qualifiers next to the claim; do not invent availability,
   delivery dates, certification, or support guarantees.
3. If status is unknown or conflicting, report the unresolved claim for review and
   withhold the unsupported statement. Do not guess PLANNED as a substitute.
4. Check that the promised job belongs to the website or is clearly described as a
   separate operational product; authenticated UI does not expand website ownership.

Include evidence/uncertainty in the change report. M0.1 establishes policy only;
do not build a capability ledger or produce a public marketing site here.
